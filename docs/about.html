SDLC Radar

A living synthesis of current AI software development, QA, and test automation trends.

Core idea

This project separates short term news from lasting understanding.

  • Daily editions
    Capture what changed on a given day.
  • Topic pages
    Synthesize repeated signals across multiple editions.
  • The dashboard
    Presents the current picture in a format that is fast to scan and useful in consulting conversations.

Evaluation language

  • Production now
  • Experiment now
  • Watch closely
  • Early research

Philosophy

The goal is not to amplify hype. The goal is to identify repeated, technically meaningful patterns and turn them into actionable guidance.
